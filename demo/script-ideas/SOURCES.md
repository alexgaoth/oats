# Sources for the second demo set

Everything in `demo/script-ideas/` is fiction: the people, the hacker house,
the startups, their numbers, their p(doom)s and their AGI timelines. It reports
no news, and names real people and companies only as culture or for the four
facts below.

The few real-world facts the conversations state were checked on 2026-10-15:

- **The bitter lesson.** Richard Sutton's essay, 13 March 2019: general
  methods that leverage computation win in the long run, and search and
  learning are the methods that keep scaling. Hacker house 19:41–22:43; GPU
  poor support group.
  - http://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html
- **Founder mode.** Paul Graham's essay "Founder Mode", September 2024, which
  contrasts founder mode with manager mode. Hacker house 12:31; pre-seed pitch.
  - https://paulgraham.com/foundermode.html
- **Vibe coding.** The term was coined by Andrej Karpathy in February 2025.
  Hacker house 7:12.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vibe_coding
- **996.** Working 9 am to 9 pm, six days a week. Hacker house 23:00; pre-seed
  pitch; Dolores Park; call with Mom.
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/996_working_hour_system

Named as culture only, with nothing claimed about them: YC and its batches,
Waymo, Slack, Dolores Park, Hayes Valley, the Mission, SoMa and Golden Gate
Park. The memes (p(doom), e/acc and decels, feel the AGI, GPU poor, cracked,
locked in, so back and so over, touch grass) are used as memes.

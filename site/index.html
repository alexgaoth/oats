High-signal tool for high-signal conversations

Oats records the conversation in the room and transcribes it on your Mac. It tracks the questions people ask and whether the room answered them, checks an unsure answer against your team's notes, and hands you the summary when you stop.

Make room for the conversation

Press ⇧⌘O in any app and talk. Oats listens through one microphone and steps aside while people speak. One line draws the conversation as it happens: its height is how loud the room is, a notch marks where the subject turned, and a mark shows where someone asked a question.

  • Transcribed on your Mac with whisper.cpp, on the GPU
  • Press M to mark a moment
  • Calm shows the line only. Words adds the live transcript

Walk out knowing what is still open

Oats hears a question and watches what the room does with it. The card says how it ended, in place, while people are still talking. A question asked twice is kept as a signal.

Answered
Someone gave an answer.
Unknown
The room said it did not know.
Unanswered
The room moved on.
Open
Nobody has replied yet.

Checked against what your team wrote down

Turn it on in Settings and choose a folder of your team's notes. When an answer in the room sounds unsure, Oats finds the passage that settles it, in that folder or in your past conversations, and says so under the question: That's right, Wait, that's wrong, or Found it when the room did not know, with the answer and the file it came from.

Optional, and off by default: when the folder has no answer, Oats can ask Claude Code to look in GitHub, Linear and Notion, read only.

The conversation, given back in order

A title, a summary and the threads the conversation followed. Talk time for each person, how each question ended, and the transcript with the speakers told apart.

Titles and summaries come from a writing model you choose: a local one, or your own API key.

Find anything anyone said

Search every transcript, part words included: rice finds price. Chinese and Japanese work too. Each search shows how many matches it found and how long it took.

  • Press / to search from anywhere in the app
  • Each match shows when in the conversation it was said

See how your conversations connect

The board lays out every conversation as a graph and as a timeline, so a subject that keeps coming back shows as the arcs between the days it came up. Each one is filed into Work, Personal or a group you make.

A local model files each conversation from its title, topics and summary, never from the transcript.

Privacy you can check

Audio and transcripts stay on your Mac. There is no Oats account and no Oats server. Summaries run on a local model, or on your own API key if you add one.

When a question meets a clear I don't know, Oats opens a web search for the question text in your browser. You can turn that off in Settings.

The sidebar counts every network request since launch. Calls to the speech, writing and search servers that run on your Mac are not counted, because they never leave it. Every destination is listed.

The first time you open it

This version is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS stops it once.

  1. Open the DMG and drag Oats to Applications.
  2. Open Oats. macOS says it cannot verify the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Oats was blocked.
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID. macOS asks only once.

Tell people before you record. Some places, for example California, need everyone's consent.
